Reciprocity Teaser

For my final manifesto project I created a teaser for my film Reciprocity. Reciprocity is a documentary following 4 Ecuadorians in New York as they address the impacts of assimilation by practicing land based rituals each season. For this teaser I used production footage of the four main characters Danna Yana Allpa (Summer), Alma Herrera-Pazmiño (Fall), Amara Ullauri (Winter), and Jasmin Duran (Spring). I started the footage with archival footage from the Runa Pacha workshop that Amara facilitated and Charlie Uruchima documented of the four participants in 2019. Also, I used a zine that Amara created for the Runa Pacha workshop as the base illustration for the animation. In Procreate I traced eight times each individual animation that appears on screen from the zine. In After Effects I created a composition loop and revealed the openning title using masks and a stroke effect. I edited all of these assets in Premiere Pro adding sound bites from the archival footage and production footage. Taking input from my classmates I hope this teaser relfects the cyclical style of storytelling that Reciprocity is. As the director of this film the teaser is meant to reflect the intentional connection to nature in an urban environment like New York. Using that time to be able to have a personal relationship with nature in order to create a space of physical belonging. Living in reciprocity requires active conciousness to intentionally connect with the land and eachother. I leave this offering here for those interested in what this can look like.
